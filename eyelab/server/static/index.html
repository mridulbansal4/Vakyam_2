Vakyam · वाक्यम् · sentence

Speak without hands.

For the next minute, imagine you cannot move or speak. Your eyes are all you have left. Answer a question with them, and hear your own sentence.

  1. Calibrate your eyes: centre, left, right, up, top left, top right, and closed. About fifteen seconds.
  2. Type with six eye gestures, exactly like the Vakyam app: each corner is a letter group, so a word takes one look per letter. Close your eyes to switch to the word list; look up to delete, or to finish.
  3. Hear the sentence Gemini writes from your words and the caregiver's question.

Frames go to the Eye Lab server on AWS, are analysed in memory with OpenCV 5, and are never stored. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, with light on your face.
